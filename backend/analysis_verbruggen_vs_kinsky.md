- Kinsky: home vs Newcastle, FPL difficulty **2** (easy)
- Verbruggen: away at Chelsea, FPL difficulty **4** (hard)

The gap (5.15 vs 4.79, ~0.36 pts) is modest — this is "narrowly favors the easier fixture," not "wildly wrong about who's good."
